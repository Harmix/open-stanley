# Open Stanley compared with the paid "AI head of content" tools

Written from the tools' own pages and reviews read in November 2026; prices change, check theirs. This page is also honest about what they do better.

## Stanley (getstanley.ai, by Stan)

Launched March 2026, built in 14 days, iMessage/Telegram-first, posts to LinkedIn, X, Instagram and more. $44.99/month in the App Store after a 3-day trial; earlier LinkedIn plans were reported around $149/month. It learns your voice from your past posts and voice notes, suggests ideas, drafts, schedules, posts and reports.

What Stanley does better: zero setup (text a number and it starts), a mobile app, a team that cold-emails creators with a personalized analysis, and Stan Store's distribution behind it. If you will not install anything, use Stanley.

What Open Stanley does that Stanley cannot: read your meetings, Slack, email and docs through the MCPs you already have; keep every draft→final edit and turn repeated edits into rules; show the receipt for every claim; run experiments on your own ledger; keep your material in a folder you own. And it costs nothing on top of the Claude plan you already pay for.

Open Stanley was built in a four-hour hackathon as a reply to Stanley, and credits it: the growth strategy inside Stanley is real work by its founders, and this plugin copies the loop and changes the parts that did not work for a founder with his material spread across tools.

## Taplio, Supergrow, AuthoredUp, Kleo and the LinkedIn-tool category

Web apps at $29–199/month: post generators from trending posts and templates, scheduling, analytics, a Chrome extension. Good at formatting and scheduling, and at showing what is trending in a niche.

What they do better: polished scheduling calendars, carousel editors, team seats, and support.

What Open Stanley does differently: it refuses to generate a post from a topic. Every draft starts from a thing that happened to you with a source, and the audit flags the patterns the 2026 feeds demote. The post is yours, not a template.

## When not to use Open Stanley

- You post for a brand page with a team and approval workflow: Oktopost's open-source Claude plugin or a scheduling tool fits better.
- You want nothing installed and no folder on your machine: Stanley.
- You need Instagram or TikTok: Open Stanley covers LinkedIn and X.
