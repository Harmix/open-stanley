# Shape vocabulary

A shape is a move another writer can make with different material. Name them consistently so `write` can ask for one and `stanley-stats` can score them.

## Openers (`opener:`)
- `event` — "Ran a quick experiment with my team this weekend." / "Someone tried to hack my GSuite today."
- `claim` — a position stated flat: "Winning deals is still hard. But the work around it has never been easier."
- `number` — the figure first: "I made $3.42M in August 2026."
- `lost-argument` — "One of the debates I've had for 15 years (and mostly lost)…"
- `one-of` — "One of my favorite books…" (low-pressure, personal)
- `past-self` — "Early on in my career, I had 10 direct reports."
- `breaking` — "BREAKING NEWS:" used ironically or literally
- `question-to-self` — a question the author is actually asking, not bait

## Bodies (`body:`)
- `single-observation` — one thought, 1–4 sentences, no structure (PG on X)
- `story-lesson` — what happened → the one lesson
- `argument-lost` — the counter-argument stated fairly, then intuition
- `steps` — numbered 1/2/3 with a concrete result at the end
- `then-now` — how it used to be / how it is now (Yamini)
- `product-episode` — "Episode N: here's how I used it today", with price (Dharmesh)
- `numbers-breakdown` — revenue/conversion/churn laid out plainly (Dodonov)
- `contrast-pair` — two runs of the same test with different outcomes (the note-taker post)

## Specificity (`specific:`)
- `exact-number` — unrounded ("1,239,964 emails")
- `named-person` — with permission or public
- `dated-event`
- `own-mistake`
- `own-inbox` — a screenshot or quote of something that actually arrived

## Endings (`ending:`)
- `flat` — last real sentence, no summary
- `intuition` — "my intuition still tells me…"
- `open-question` — one specific question a peer could answer
- `cta-price` — "$10/month, cancel anytime"
- `teaser` — "more on 09.09"
- `ps` — a p.s. that carries a joke or the real point

## Form (`form:`)
- `length:<band>` (aphorism <200c / claim 200–900c / story 900–2000c / essay >2000c)
- `paragraphs:<1-line|2-3 sentences|mixed>`
- `media:<none|screenshot|photo|document|video>`
- `punct:<em-dash|parens|ps|none>`

## Never (`never:`)
- record explicitly what the creator does not do: hashtags, engagement questions, emoji, bold lists
