# Yuriy Zaremba, 45 LinkedIn posts

Yuriy Zaremba is the CEO of AiSDR, an AI agent that writes and sends outbound sales emails, and he has 34,018 followers on LinkedIn. He posts every day, weekends included. The 45 posts I read on November 21 cover October 8 to November 20. Most are plain text of about 140 words, about the AI business he is building or about something that happened in his day.

I sorted the posts into four kinds: posts that open on a number from AiSDR, launches and asks, posts about his day, and his takes on AI and the market. Then I put all of them on one chart.
