# James Hawkins, 45 LinkedIn posts

James Hawkins is a co-founder of PostHog, a product analytics tool for developers, and he has 65,287 followers on LinkedIn. Almost every post he writes is a joke about startups or AI. None of his own captions in this set mention PostHog by name.

On November 9 I read his last 45 posts. They cover less than three weeks, because he posts twice a day: once between 7 and 9 in the morning Pacific time and once between 10 and noon, usually a minute or two after the hour. I sorted the posts by how they are built and put all of them on one chart.
