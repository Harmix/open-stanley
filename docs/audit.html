Does this post sound like AI?

This is the check the Open Stanley plugin runs on every draft, running in your browser instead. Nothing you paste leaves the page. It looks for the measured tells: the words that appear far more often in model text (Kobak et al. 2024, Juzek and Ward 2025), the sentence shapes readers flag, and the LinkedIn patterns the 226K-post study found are demoted. It is a list of rules, not a detector's guess, so it can tell you which line to fix.

What the flags mean

  • blockA pattern that reads as generated, or one LinkedIn's 2026 ranking demotes: hashtags, a link in the body, an explicit like or share ask. Fix it before posting.
  • warnFine once, generated when it repeats: a rule-of-three list, an em dash, a generic closing question. Your call.
  • infoContext, not a verdict: a time-relative claim to date-check, a length note, a framing that cost reach in the study.
  • landingEight yes or no items that went with reach in the 226K-post study: opens in first person, a complete thought before the fold, a name people know early, a number, a closing question, an image, 400 to 1,900 characters, no links or hashtags.

The full script, skills/stanley/scripts/stanley-audit in the repo, also checks every number against your own story files and every draft against your past posts for overlap. Those two need the vault, so they run only in the plugin. The research behind the rules is in RESEARCH.md.
