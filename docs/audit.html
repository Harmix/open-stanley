Does this post sound like AI?

The same audit the Open Stanley plugin runs on every draft, ported to run in your browser. Nothing you paste leaves this page. It checks the measured tells (excess AI vocabulary from Kobak et al. 2024 and Juzek & Ward 2025, the structures readers flag, and the LinkedIn patterns the 226K-post study found are demoted), not a detector's guess.

What the flags mean

BLOCK is a pattern that reads generated or that LinkedIn's 2026 ranking demotes (hashtags, link in the body, explicit like/share asks). WARN is a pattern that is fine once and generated when it repeats. INFO is context. The landing line is eight yes/no items that correlated with reach in the 226K-post study: opening in first person, a complete thought before the fold, a name people know early, a number, a closing question, an image, 400–1,900 characters, no links or hashtags.

The full script, with the receipts check against your own story files and the self-overlap check against your past posts, is skills/stanley/scripts/stanley-audit in the repo. The research it encodes is in RESEARCH.md.
