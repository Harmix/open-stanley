# Stanley alternatives in 2026

Most "Stanley alternatives" lists are written by one of the alternatives, and the author's tool comes first. This one is too: I built Open Stanley. So I put the other tools first, and for each one I wrote what it does better than mine. I read their own pages, their MCP docs and reviews by other people on 7 November 2026. Prices change, so check theirs.

## <svg class="orange" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><path d="M4 6h16M4 12h16M4 18h16"/></svg>The short version

| Tool | Price | Good at | Pick it if |
|---|---|---|---|
| Taplio | $39 to $199 a month | Scheduling calendar, reach prediction, a feed of viral posts in your niche, carousels, lead lists | You want one big LinkedIn suite and a calendar |
| Supergrow | $29 to $99 a month | AI interviews that turn your answers into posts, a Kanban board, teams and approvals | You post with a team or for a brand |
| AuthoredUp | $20 to $40 a month | The best LinkedIn editor and preview, formatting, snippets, analytics | You write yourself and want a better editor |
| Kleo | Free Chrome extension | Shows a creator's best posts | You want to study other people's posts |
| PostAgent, 2PR | Paid, hosted | Agents that learn from your material and check claims | You want this kind of agent hosted for you |
| Stanley itself | $44.99 a month (iOS) | Zero setup, text it from your phone, native publishing to X, LinkedIn, Threads, Instagram, Substack Notes | You will not install anything |
| Open Stanley | Free, MIT | Posts from your own meetings, Slack, mail and notes, each claim with its source, edits saved as rules | You use Claude and want your material in a folder you own |

## <svg class="rose" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><path d="M12 3l2.7 5.5 6 .9-4.4 4.2 1 6-5.3-2.8-5.3 2.8 1-6L3.3 9.4l6-.9z"/></svg>Why people leave Stanley

I paid for Stanley from August 2026 and used it every day in early September, then cancelled. The comment scans were fine. The posts were the problem: Stanley did not know what happened in my week, because my meetings live in a tool it could not read. On the first day it gave me eight drafts and I rejected seven. It suggested I comment on my own post and cited a 2024 paper as news. If your stories are in your meetings and chats, a tool that writes from a topic will keep guessing.

## <svg class="green" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><path d="M20 6L9 17l-5-5"/></svg>What changed this autumn

Stanley, Taplio and Supergrow all have an MCP server now, so "it works from Claude" is no longer a difference. What is left: what the tool reads, where your material lives, whether your edits turn into rules, whether a number in a draft has a source, and the price.
