# Open Stanley compared

One page per tool, written from their own pages and MCP docs, with the cases where they win stated plainly. The full comparison with the roadmap it produced is [compare.md](https://github.com/Harmix/open-stanley/blob/main/open-stanley/docs/compare.md) in the repo.

- [Open Stanley vs Stanley AI](stanley-alternative.html): the tool this one replies to; $44.99–149 a month, hosted, Instagram and Threads.
- [Open Stanley vs Taplio](taplio-alternative.html): the viral-post feed, reach prediction and queue at $39–199 a month.
- [Open Stanley vs Supergrow](supergrow-alternative.html): the AI interview, teams and a board from $29 a month.

What all three now share with Open Stanley: an MCP or plugin, so each works from Claude. What none of them does: keep your material in a folder you own, turn your edits into rules you can read, attach a receipt to every claim, and run experiments on your own numbers.
