# Open Stanley vs Supergrow

Supergrow's best idea is the interview: an AI asks you questions and drafts from your answers. Open Stanley's morning Soapbox question is the same idea, grounded in your week. This page says where they part. Facts from Supergrow's pages read on 7 November 2026; prices change.

## <svg class="orange" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><path d="M4 6h16M4 12h16M4 18h16"/></svg>The short version

| | Supergrow | Open Stanley |
|---|---|---|
| Price | From $29 a month; 7-day trial | $0 on top of the Claude plan you have |
| The interview | PostCast: an AI interviewer with a question bank | One question a morning about a real story from your meetings, calendar or Slack; you answer by voice note or two lines |
| Drafting | Content DNA, repurposing, voice-to-post | Edits your own words and sources into a post; every claim has a receipt |
| Scheduling | Queue, calendar, first-comment scheduling | LinkedIn's own scheduler through your browser; first-comment links handled |
| Content management | Kanban board | A folder of stories, drafts and a ledger, readable in any editor |
| Engagement | Focus Mode, AI comments | Scout drafts 1–3 sentence comments; you paste |
| Teams | Feedback and approval, org management, leaderboards | Single user |
| Free tools | Hook, headline, carousel, post preview generators | A browser audit for AI tells and demotion signals ([try it](../audit.html)); the preview renderer ships in the plugin |
| Where your material lives | Their servers | A folder you own |
| MCP | Yes | Is a plugin: Cowork, Claude Code, any SKILL.md agent |

## <svg class="rose" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><path d="M12 3l2.7 5.5 6 .9-4.4 4.2 1 6-5.3-2.8-5.3 2.8 1-6L3.3 9.4l6-.9z"/></svg>Choose Supergrow if

- You run a team and need approval workflows and a board.
- You want a hosted app with support and a 4.8 on G2.
- You like the interview format and want a question bank, not your calendar, to drive it.

## <svg class="green" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><path d="M20 6L9 17l-5-5"/></svg>Choose Open Stanley if

- You want the question to come from something that happened to you this week, and the answer filed with its source before anything is drafted.
- You want the linter and the experiments, not just the editor.
- You want nothing of yours on a server you do not control, and no new subscription.

## <svg class="sky" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><path d="M12 3v12m-5-5 5 5 5-5M4 21h16"/></svg>Install

Cowork: Plugins → Add marketplace → `Harmix/open-stanley`. Claude Code: `/plugin marketplace add Harmix/open-stanley` then `/plugin install open-stanley@open-stanley`. Other agents: `npx skills add Harmix/open-stanley`. Source: [github.com/Harmix/open-stanley](https://github.com/Harmix/open-stanley).
